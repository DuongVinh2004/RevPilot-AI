## 1. Principles of Governed Hypothesis Generation and Evaluation

In RevPilot AI, a **Hypothesis** is a structured, falsifiable explanatory candidate for an observed business anomaly. Hypotheses are not generative opinions or ungrounded model text; they are versioned domain aggregates linked to immutable evidence items, derived statistics, and causal studies.

### 1.1 Non-Negotiable Invariants
1. **Multi-Hypothesis Competition (`FR-RCA-001`, `AC-004`)**: Investigations must evaluate competing, mutually distinct candidate explanations for every detected anomaly. Single-hypothesis linear assertions are invalid.
2. **Dual Evidence Binding (`FR-RCA-001`)**: Every hypothesis must explicitly evaluate both **supporting evidence** and **contradicting evidence**. Contradicting facts cannot be omitted or suppressed.
3. **Strict Epistemic Taxonomy**: The system must maintain a strict ontological distinction between:
   - *Observation*: Directly measured ground fact (e.g. order cancelled at timestamp $T$).
   - *Derived Statistic*: Deterministic mathematical aggregation (e.g. cancellation rate increased by 6.6%).
   - *Association / Correlation*: Statistically observed co-movement without directional proof (e.g. cancellations correlate with marketing campaign).
   - *Mechanism Hypothesis*: Structural narrative explaining the transmission channel (e.g. warehouse backlog caused delivery delays, triggering cancellations).
   - *Statistical Signal*: Anomaly detection alert or change-point score.
   - *Causal Hypothesis*: Specific proposition that variable $X$ has a non-zero average treatment effect on outcome $Y$.
   - *Causal Estimate*: Point estimate with confidence intervals derived from identified observational or experimental estimators.
   - *Recommendation*: Proposed business intervention (strictly separated from hypothesis; Phase 05/06 scope).
4. **No Spurious Probability or Hallucinated Confidence (`INV-AI-001`)**: Raw model generation likelihoods must never be represented as calibrated statistical probabilities. Ranking scores are relative ordinal metrics unless backed by formal conformal prediction or Bayesian posterior calibration.
5. **Deterministic Fallback to `NEED_MORE_EVIDENCE` (`FR-RCA-002`)**: If evidence is contradictory, missing on critical dimensions, or below confidence thresholds, the system must output `NEED_MORE_EVIDENCE`. It is forbidden to force a conclusive verdict without evidence.
6. **No Action Authority (`INV-ACT-001`)**: Hypotheses and verifier results are read-only analytical artifacts. They cannot trigger tools, grant authorizations, or initiate external actions.

## 2. Canonical Hypothesis Data Contract

Every hypothesis conforms to the typed `HypothesisRecord` schema:

```python
class HypothesisType(str, Enum):
    LOGISTICS_BOTTLENECK = "LOGISTICS_BOTTLENECK"
    PAYMENT_GATEWAY_OUTAGE = "PAYMENT_GATEWAY_OUTAGE"
    PRODUCT_QUALITY_DEFECT = "PRODUCT_QUALITY_DEFECT"
    PRICING_CATALOG_MISCONFIGURATION = "PRICING_CATALOG_MISCONFIGURATION"
    EXTERNAL_MACRO_EVENT = "EXTERNAL_MACRO_EVENT"
    CUSTOMER_BEHAVIOR_SHIFT = "CUSTOMER_BEHAVIOR_SHIFT"

class HypothesisStatus(str, Enum):
    PROPOSED = "PROPOSED"             # Draft hypothesis generated by synthesizer
    EVALUATING = "EVALUATING"         # Gathering evidence and checking claims
    VERIFIED = "VERIFIED"             # Formally supported by evidence; no refuting evidence
    PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED = "PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED" # Plausible mechanism, incomplete data
    REFUTED = "REFUTED"               # Actively contradicted by authoritative evidence
    NEED_MORE_EVIDENCE = "NEED_MORE_EVIDENCE"   # Critical data gaps prevent ranking

class EpistemicCategory(str, Enum):
    OBSERVATION = "OBSERVATION"
    DERIVED_STATISTIC = "DERIVED_STATISTIC"
    ASSOCIATION = "ASSOCIATION"
    MECHANISM_HYPOTHESIS = "MECHANISM_HYPOTHESIS"
    STATISTICAL_SIGNAL = "STATISTICAL_SIGNAL"
    CAUSAL_HYPOTHESIS = "CAUSAL_HYPOTHESIS"
    CAUSAL_ESTIMATE = "CAUSAL_ESTIMATE"
    RECOMMENDATION = "RECOMMENDATION"

class EvidenceWeight(BaseModel):
    evidence_id: UUIDv7
    content_digest: str
    relevance_score: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)
    polarity: Literal["SUPPORTING", "CONTRADICTING"]
    citation_span_ref: Optional[str] = None
    provenance_source: str

class HypothesisRecord(BaseModel):
    hypothesis_id: str                # E.g. "hypo_midwest_capacity_01"
    investigation_id: UUIDv7
    tenant_id: TenantId
    statement: str
    epistemic_category: EpistemicCategory = EpistemicCategory.MECHANISM_HYPOTHESIS
    hypothesis_type: HypothesisType
    affected_scope: Dict[str, Any]    # E.g. {"region": "US-MIDWEST", "facility": "WH-MIDWEST-01"}
    time_window_start: UtcDateTime
    time_window_end: UtcDateTime
    supporting_evidence: List[EvidenceWeight]
    contradicting_evidence: List[EvidenceWeight]
    evidence_coverage_ratio: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)
    ordinal_rank: int = Field(ge=1)
    ranking_score: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0) # Relative heuristic score
    ranking_method: Literal["EVIDENCE_EVALUATION_V1", "CAUSAL_STUDY_BACKED"]
    alternative_hypothesis_ids: List[str]
    missing_evidence_descriptors: List[str]
    limitations: List[str]
    status: HypothesisStatus
    causal_study_id: Optional[UUIDv7] = None
    created_at: UtcDateTime
    updated_at: UtcDateTime
    manifest_digest: str              # SHA-256 over canonical fields
```

## 3. Competing-Cause Ranking Engine

The ranking engine evaluates candidate hypotheses against gathered evidence bundles using a multi-criteria scoring algorithm:

$$\text{Score}(H) = w_{\text{sup}} \cdot S_{\text{sup}}(H) - w_{\text{con}} \cdot S_{\text{con}}(H) + w_{\text{causal}} \cdot S_{\text{causal}}(H) - w_{\text{gap}} \cdot G(H)$$

Where:
- $S_{\text{sup}}(H)$: Normalized coverage and quality score of supporting evidence.
- $S_{\text{con}}(H)$: Penalization for authoritative contradicting evidence.
- $S_{\text{causal}}(H)$: Bonus if backed by a validated `CausalStudy` with identified effect direction and interval.
- $G(H)$: Missing evidence penalty based on unresolved dimensions.
- Default weights: $w_{\text{sup}} = 0.40, w_{\text{con}} = 0.45, w_{\text{causal}} = 0.30, w_{\text{gap}} = 0.25$.

### Ranking Invariants:
1. **Contradiction Veto**: If an authoritative contradiction exists ($S_{\text{con}} \ge 0.80$), the hypothesis status MUST be `REFUTED`, and its rank is penalized below all non-refuted candidates.
2. **Co-Equal Ranking**: If two hypotheses have score difference $\le 0.05$ with distinct mechanisms, they are marked co-equal (tie), and the verifier flags `AMBIGUOUS_COMPETING_CAUSES`.
3. **No Fabricated Top-1**: The top-ranked hypothesis cannot be declared `VERIFIED` unless:
   - Supporting evidence coverage $\ge 0.75$.
   - Contradicting evidence $= 0$ authoritative items.
   - At least 2 distinct competing hypotheses have been evaluated and refuted or downgraded.
   - Otherwise, verdict transitions to `NEED_MORE_EVIDENCE` (`FR-RCA-002`).

## 4. Midwest Truck-Capacity Anomaly Case Study (Benchmarking Reference)

Under `SYNTHETIC-DATASET-SPEC.md` §6:
1. **Leading Hypothesis ($H_1$)**: `TRUCK_CAPACITY_SHORTAGE_MIDWEST`
   - *Statement*: Fleet shortage at regional carrier caused dispatch backlog at `WH-MIDWEST-01`, leading to shipment delays and customer cancellations under 48-hour delivery guarantee.
   - *Supporting Evidence*: Carrier dispatch latency metrics (spike to 84h), ticket volume on delay topics (4.5x rise), warehouse backlog table.
   - *Contradicting Evidence*: None.
   - *Status*: `VERIFIED` (Rank 1).
2. **Decoy Hypothesis 1 ($H_2$)**: `PAYMENT_GATEWAY_OUTAGE`
   - *Statement*: Checkout payment failure caused checkout drop-off and cancellations.
   - *Supporting Evidence*: None.
   - *Contradicting Evidence*: `payment_failure_rate` steady at 1.2% across incident window (`METRIC-005`).
   - *Status*: `REFUTED` (Contradiction identified).
3. **Decoy Hypothesis 2 ($H_3$)**: `PRODUCT_QUALITY_DEFECT`
   - *Statement*: SKU-8899 batch defects prompted customer cancellations and returns.
   - *Supporting Evidence*: Return volume slightly elevated.
   - *Contradicting Evidence*: Return reasons show 89% `CUSTOMER_REQUEST_DELAY` rather than defective item.
   - *Status*: `REFUTED` (Contradiction identified).
