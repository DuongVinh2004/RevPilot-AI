## Adjudication rules

- Reports are candidate inputs, not authority.
- Current repository evidence, the supplied safety policy, accepted ADRs, explicit invariants, approved requirements/NFRs, the Definition of Done, and traceable test/evidence records control the decision.
- A range in the PRD is treated as planned scope, not as a fully defined SRS requirement, until an individual requirement contract exists.
- `Designed`, `Planned`, `UNKNOWN`, `PENDING`, and `NOT EXECUTED` are never promoted to measured or accepted evidence.
- Severity was recalibrated to the requested P0–P4 definitions. No documentation-only issue was promoted to P0 without evidence of direct unsafe system behavior or data loss.
