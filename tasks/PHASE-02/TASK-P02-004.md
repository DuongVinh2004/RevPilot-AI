## Objective and rationale

OBJECTIVE: Implement the multi-dimensional anomaly localization and drill-down engine in `packages/backend/src/revpilot/modules/analytics/localization.py` per `docs/02-domain/ANOMALY-LOCALIZATION-SPEC.md`, ranking top contributing segments, enforcing minimum sample sizes ($d_k \ge 30$), applying Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control, and producing associative (non-causal) diagnostic summaries.

BUSINESS RATIONALE: Provide analysts and downstream investigation workflows with immediate visibility into which operational segments concentrate the anomaly, while strictly preventing misleading causal claims (`BR-001`, `FR-DET-004`, `FR-INV-004`, `INV-TEN-001`, `INV-AI-001`, `NFR-AI-004`, `AC-002`).

## Symbol-level contract

MUST EXPORT/DEFINE:
- `SegmentContribution(rank: int, segment_value: str, actual_value: float, baseline_expected_value: float, relative_lift: float, sample_size: int, excess_volume: float, contribution_pct: float, p_value: float, fdr_rejected: bool)`
- `AnomalyLocalizationResult(localization_id: str, anomaly_id: str, tenant_id: TenantId, dimension: str, top_contributing_segments: List[SegmentContribution], suppressed_segments_count: int, association_summary: str, diagnostic_status: str)`
- `AnomalyLocalizer`:
  - `localize(anomaly: AnomalyRecord, dimension: str, secondary_dimension: Optional[str] = None, tenant_context: TenantContext) -> Result[AnomalyLocalizationResult, LocalizationError]`

MUST NOT:
- Include causal claims (such as "caused by" or "root cause is") in `association_summary`.
- Rank segments with sample size $< 30$ in `top_contributing_segments`.
- Allow drill-down dimensions outside `METRIC-REGISTRY.md` allowed dimensions.
