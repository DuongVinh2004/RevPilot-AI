## Symbol-level contract

MUST EXPORT/DEFINE:
- `AnomalyState`: Enum (`DETECTED`, `VALIDATED`, `LOCALIZED`, `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `SUPPRESSED`, `RESOLVED`, `REOPENED`)
- `AnomalyTransitionRecord(transition_id: str, tenant_id: TenantId, anomaly_id: str, from_state: AnomalyState, to_state: AnomalyState, actor_id: str, actor_type: str, reason: str, metadata: Dict[str, Any], transitioned_at: UtcDateTime)`
- `AnomalyAggregate`:
  - `transition_to(target_state: AnomalyState, actor: PrincipalContext, reason: str, metadata: Dict[str, Any]) -> Result[AnomalyTransitionRecord, TransitionError]`
- `ReconciliationEngine`:
  - `reconcile_window(tenant_id: TenantId, metric_id: str, affected_window: TimeWindow) -> List[AnomalyAggregate]`

MUST NOT:
- Permit illegal transitions (e.g. `DETECTED -> RESOLVED` without validation/acknowledgement).
- Overwrite or delete prior anomaly state in place; supersession sets `effective_to` and transitions to `SUPPRESSED`.
- Publish outbox events outside the atomic transition boundary.

---

## Input/output and validation

INPUT: Current `AnomalyAggregate`, target state, actor context, transition reason.
OUTPUT: Updated aggregate, persisted transition record, and queued outbox event.
VALIDATION:
- Illegal transition raises `Err(INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION)`.
- Recomputation shifting metric below threshold transitions anomaly to `SUPPRESSED` with reason `SUPERSEDED_BY_RECOMPUTATION`.
