## 7. Failure, Retry, Timeout, and Idempotency

- Verifier evaluation is pure and deterministic. Execution time <= 50ms per claim.
- Any violation of the 11 rejection triggers immediately assigns `UNSUPPORTED` or `CONTRADICTED` with explicit `rejection_reason`.
- Verification results are idempotent and cached against `(claim_id, evidence_bundle_digest, as_of_time)`.

## 8. Security, Tenancy, and Privacy Contract

- Enforce `INV-AI-001`: 0.00% unsupported claims allowed in verified hypotheses.
- Enforce `INV-DATA-001`: Claims citing records timestamped > `:as_of_time` are rejected with `ERR_TEMPORAL_LEAKAGE`.
- Enforce `INV-SEC-002`: Untrusted agent text cannot override verifier rules or bypass citation checks.

- [ ] Verifier classifies claims into 6 formal categories deterministically.

- [ ] Statements directly contradicted by canonical database receive status `CONTRADICTED`.
