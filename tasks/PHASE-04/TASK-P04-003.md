## 7. Failure, Retry, Timeout, and Idempotency

- Inclusion of post-treatment variables in the covariate set raises `ERR_POST_TREATMENT_COLLIDER` and rejects study registration.
- Studies with temporal lookahead (> `:as_of_time`) raise `ERR_TEMPORAL_LEAKAGE`.
- Creation of duplicate studies under same `idempotency_key` returns existing study.

---

## 8. Security, Tenancy, and Privacy Contract

- Enforce `INV-TEN-001..003`: `CausalStudy` contains `tenant_id`. All data extractions inject server-derived tenant filter.
- Enforce `INV-PRV-001`: Raw PII fields (names, emails, credit cards) cannot be registered as covariates.
- Enforce `AC-006`: Assumptions (positivity, ignorability, SUTVA) must be explicitly stated in the study contract.
