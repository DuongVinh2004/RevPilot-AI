OBJECTIVE: Implement CATE uplift modeling estimators (T-Learner, X-Learner), overlap and positivity diagnostics, Qini metric evaluation, and negative effect ("sleeping dog") detection.
BUSINESS RATIONALE: Interventions should only be targeted to customer accounts where the expected treatment effect is measurably positive ($\tau(X_i) > 0$), preventing wasted spend on lost causes and harm to sleeping dogs (`BR-002`, `FR-ML-003`, `FR-ML-004`).
ARCHITECTURAL OWNER: Causal AI Engineering (`revpilot.modules.ml.uplift`)
ARCHITECTURAL CONTEXT: Consumes synthetic randomized trial datasets. Produces `UpliftScoreRecord` with verified Qini $> 0.2000$ (`NFR-AI-006`).

## Non-functional and security invariants

- `FR-ML-003`: Uplift score represents incremental effect $\mathbb{E}[Y(1) - Y(0) \mid X]$, strictly decoupled from baseline churn risk.
- `AC-006`: Overlap requirement enforced ($0.05 \le e(X) \le 0.95$); violation halts with `ERR_OVERLAP_VIOLATION`.
- `NFR-AI-006`: Uplift model achieves normalized Qini $> 0.2000$ and AUUC beating random baseline by $\ge 15.0\%$.
- Negative Effect Guard: Accounts flagged as "Sleeping Dogs" ($\tau(X_i) < 0$) are categorized and blocked from intervention.
