## Non-functional and security invariants

- `AC-014`: No unmeasured or fabricated compliance claims. Generates empirical metrics only.
- Small-Sample Guard: Subgroups with $N < 50$ are marked `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE` and suppressed from disparity ratios.
- `NFR-AI-001`: Re-running decision optimizer on identical input and seed produces identical SHA-256 decision digest.
- `INV-PRV-001`: Slice dimensions are restricted to business operational tiers (e.g. SMB, Enterprise); demographic PII is barred.

## Rollback and recovery

If slice evaluation detects calibration disparity $> 0.0300$ across enterprise vs SMB tiers, promotion to production is blocked (`GOVERNANCE_STATUS = NEEDS_REVIEW`).
