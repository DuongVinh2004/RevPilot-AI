## Non-functional and security invariants

- `INV-AUD-001`: 100% of approved, rejected, dispatched, completed, and compensated actions emit structured, unsampled audit logs.
- Outcome Segregation: Observed action outcomes cannot automatically trigger model promotion or policy relaxation (`INV-AI-001`).
- Event Contract: Outbox events strictly conform to JSON schemas defined in `EVENT-CONTRACTS.md` §8.
