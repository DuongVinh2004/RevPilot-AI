## Objective and rationale

OBJECTIVE: Implement commercial B2B SaaS connector adapters (`SalesforceConnectorAdapter`, `StripeConnectorAdapter`, `ZendeskConnectorAdapter`), Ephemeral Credential Broker, and Governed Forward Egress Proxy Gateway conforming to `docs/17-connectors/CONNECTOR-PLATFORM-SPEC.md`, `ADR-0009`, `DEC-001`, `DEC-006`, and `DEC-007`.
BUSINESS RATIONALE: Provide enterprise revenue intelligence integration across CRM (Salesforce), Billing (Stripe), and Support (Zendesk) systems while strictly enforcing ephemeral credential boundaries (TTL <= 15m), zero secret exposure (`INV-SEC-001`), domain containment (`INV-SEC-003`), and multi-tenant isolation (`INV-TEN-001`).
ARCHITECTURAL OWNER: Principal Platform Architect & Security Architect
ARCHITECTURAL CONTEXT: Conforms to `CONNECTOR-PLATFORM-SPEC.md`, `SECURITY-ARCHITECTURE.md` §4/§7.1, `CANONICAL-DATA-MODEL.md`, and `ADR-0009`.

## Scope

- In-Scope: BaseConnectorAdapter contract, Salesforce CRM adapter (Customer and Contract mapping), Stripe Billing adapter (HMAC-SHA256 signature verification, DEC-006 2-tier deduplication, PaymentReference mapping), Zendesk Support adapter (SupportTicket mapping and SLA breach classification), Ephemeral Credential Broker (DEC-007 <= 15m TTL enforcement, zero raw secret exposure), Governed Egress Proxy Gateway (INV-SEC-003 domain allowlist enforcement and SSRF defense).
- Non-Goals: Live external socket connections during testing; long-lived vendor API tokens in worker runtimes.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `AC-P08-011-01`: Salesforce adapter transforms raw CRM accounts and contracts into canonical Customer and Contract entities with tenant isolation.
2. `AC-P08-011-02`: Stripe adapter verifies cryptographic HMAC signatures, deduplicates replay events via transactional inbox (DEC-006), and maps to PaymentReference.
3. `AC-P08-011-03`: Zendesk adapter maps raw ticket records to canonical SupportTicket entities with priority grading and tenant boundary checks.
4. `AC-P08-011-04`: Credential Broker enforces maximum 15-minute token TTL (`ERR_EXCESSIVE_TOKEN_TTL` on violation per DEC-007) and redacts raw secrets in representation.
5. `AC-P08-011-05`: Governed Egress Proxy permits authorized B2B SaaS endpoints (`api.stripe.com`, `*.salesforce.com`, `*.zendesk.com`) while blocking unauthorized hosts and SSRF attempts fail-closed (`ERR_EGRESS_BLOCKED` per INV-SEC-003).
