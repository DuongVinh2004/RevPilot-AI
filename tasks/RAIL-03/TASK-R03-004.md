## Symbol-level contract

MUST TEST/VERIFY:
1. `INV-IAM-001`: Deny-by-default on missing token, empty string, or whitespace token.
2. Token signature verification: Forged tokens or tokens not present in adapter reject with `AuthenticationError`.
3. Issuer & Audience mismatch: Tokens with incorrect issuer or audience fail closed with `AuthenticationError`.
4. Token Expiry: Tokens past expiration timestamp (accounting for 60s clock skew) reject with `AuthenticationError`.
5. Token Not-Yet-Valid: Tokens with future `nbf` timestamp reject with `AuthenticationError`.
6. Revocation: Revoked sessions fail closed immediately upon lookup.
7. `INV-TEN-003`: Non-system users without tenant association reject with `TenancyViolationError`.
8. Tenant status integration: Users with tokens mapped to suspended or deactivated tenants fail closed with `TenancyViolationError`.
9. `INV-SEC-001`: Verified Principal objects and contexts expose no raw secrets, keys, or password hashes.
10. PrivilegedContext and `is_system` injection: Structural impossibility of client/token manufacturing privileged system context.
