## Symbol-level contract

MUST TEST/VERIFY:
1. `INV-IAM-001`: Deny-by-default for unassigned roles or missing permissions.
2. Cross-Tenant Isolation: Principal of Tenant A attempting to access Tenant B resource is denied.
3. Privilege Escalation: Delegator with `ANALYST` role cannot delegate `OPERATOR` capabilities.
4. Agent Approval Prohibition (`INV-ACT-003`): Delegation cannot grant `approval:grant` or `approval:*`.
5. Delegation Expiry: Expired delegation token is rejected at authorization check.
6. Delegation Revocation: Revoked delegation token is rejected at authorization check.
7. Capability & Target Boundaries: Delegation token attempting an unauthorized capability or out-of-scope target resource is denied.
8. Sub-Delegation Prohibition: Agent identity attempting to issue a new delegation token is rejected.
9. Break-Glass Misuse (`INV-TEN-003`): Non-human principal attempting break-glass raises `AuthorizationError`; expired break-glass context is rejected.
10. Policy Uncertainty (`INV-REL-001`): Simulated policy engine failure or exception denies access (fails closed).
